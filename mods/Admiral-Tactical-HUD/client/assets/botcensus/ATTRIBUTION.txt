The files in this directory are unmodified BotCensus population glyphs. Admiral Tactical HUD uses them for extended Full Census roles and as fallback assets. The original Admiral Tactical HUD atlas remains authoritative for the compact PMC, Scav, Boss and reinforced-enemy roles.

Full Census adapts the upstream vanilla classification table, known custom-faction range fallback, MoreBotsAPI registry discovery and custom boss/escort shaping. It also preserves the upstream Fika shared-player semantics through a reflection-only optional adapter. The adapters fail closed to the local SPT player list and known role ranges so neither third-party mod is mandatory.
