# Consolidated v1.1 requirement truth

Authority: Issue #338 and existing PR #341, user amendments of 2026-09-11. These supersede the old separate Item Valuation boundary and presentation-first ordering. Stable v1.0 artifacts and compatibility identifiers remain unchanged pending physical acceptance.

## Counting contract

Keep is the total unfulfilled consumptive obligation before inventory allocation. Keep-owned is the subset of currently owned stock reserved to satisfy it. Need More is the remaining shortfall. Enough means the obligations are covered but reserved stock must still be kept. Not Needed means no enabled obligation remains. Surplus can coexist with a FIR deficit when the excess stock is non-FIR.

Allocate current FIR-only demand, then future FIR-only demand. Only afterwards allocate unrestricted current quests, hideout and unrestricted future quests, using non-FIR first. Stock is never reused across these consumptive groups. The immutable allocation belongs to the same snapshot as requirements and prices; the legacy FIR registry cannot override a published tooltip generation.

Independent future quests sum even if their display names match. Explicit alternative groups select one maximum obligation consistently in both source and Keep totals. Completed conditions are excluded; partial TaskConditionCounters are subtracted only for the matching quest. Distinct handover/placement conditions remain additive; repeated condition IDs and repeated targets are deduplicated. A matching handover/placement owns the reserve for observational Find objectives. Beacon placement is consumptive.

Hideout excludes completed levels and already-paid constructing levels. Standard/custom duplicate station/level records are counted once. Remaining station levels are shown as current (next level) or future. Inventory totals use the PMC snapshot inventory, including stack counts, and FIR comes from SpawnedInSession. These are snapshot counts, not a separate live On You feature.

## Validation

The deterministic harness exhaustively checks small inventory/demand combinations for minimum feasible shortfall, FIR priority, stock conservation, source reconciliation and Keep quantities. Additional tests cover additive future demand, partial completion, duplicate hideout records, completed/current/future levels, order invariance, module-source filtering and generation isolation. Historical tests that asserted maximum future reserves or non-FIR fulfillment of FIR quests are replaced with the corrected product contract.

The executable catches test failures, reports them to stderr and exits with code 1; assertions must not produce Windows crash dialogs.

## Remaining acceptance

This foundation is not consolidated runtime acceptance. F12 lifecycle controls, original/native crisp marker, compact card hierarchy, optional background ownership/migration, useful overlap absorption, refresh lifecycle and the batched physical gate remain required. No claim of physical PASS or replacement of stable packages is made by automated tests.
