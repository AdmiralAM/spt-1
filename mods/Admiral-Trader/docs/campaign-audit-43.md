# Admiral Trader: authoritative 43-quest campaign audit

> Current audit snapshot: branch `feature/admiral-trader-canonical-milestones`, runtime baseline SPT 4.1.5, 43 committed quest templates. The four campaign corrections remain materialized, and M5 now adds relationship progression through the existing Admiral loyalty and assortment systems. Quest IDs, prerequisites, graph shape, runtime architecture and frozen 0.1.0 authority remain unchanged.

## Executive findings

The campaign contains 10 Access records, 21 Arsenal records, and 12 Operations records. It awards **483 500 XP, ₽1 997 000, and +0.829 Admiral standing** before valuing seven ammunition samples. Eight quest-assort offers unlock: one Labs access-card offer after Access Protocol: Clearance and seven ammunition offers after the seven Munitions finales. M5 adds no quests or graph branches. It adds three finite loyalty-gated Relationship offers and one requester-local replenishment projection for the existing field-marker offer.

## M5 relationship storefront

Admiral's existing loyalty thresholds are the progression authority: LL2 requires level 15 and 0.10 standing, LL3 requires level 25 and 0.30 standing, and LL4 requires level 35 and 0.55 standing. Sales volume and quest completion never substitute for those paired requirements.

The existing MS2000 marker offer keeps ID `ad2000000000000000000004`, TPL `5991b51486f77447b112d44f` and price ₽16 500. Its requester-local stock / personal limit progresses as 12/4, 16/6, 20/8 and 24/10 from LL1 through LL4. The runtime post-processes only SPT's profile-scoped assort response and never mutates the global trader assort.

The Access line is a retained-item readiness check: every key condition is non-FIR and non-consuming. The Arsenal line is seven independent three-step family chains. The Operations line supplies the actual campaign narrative and cross-links Access and Arsenal progress into map-specific field tasks.

Reward totals remain inside the repository policy ceilings. No quest is clearly over-rewarded on nominal payouts. Seven high-variance one-session Operations received bounded corrections, while the other five Operations and all Access/Arsenal cash and reputation values remain unchanged. The 49 selected weapons are now partitioned into disjoint Qualification, Fieldwork, and Munitions pools, so every selected template appears in exactly one stage.

## Method and normalization

The audit reads the committed quest JSON, English locale objective text, `questassort.json`, trader assort, the exact SPT 4.1.5 item database, and the pinned vanilla reward benchmark in `reward-policy.json`. The vanilla sample contains 558 quests. Reference medians are: overall 12 000 XP / ₽32 000 / +0.02 standing; Elimination 18 200 XP / ₽65 000; Completion 9 200 XP / ₽35 000; PickUp 8 350 XP / ₽15 000; Exploration 10 000 XP / ₽40 000. Overall p75/p90 are 22 500/39 930 XP, ₽85 000/₽170 600, and +0.03/+0.04 standing.

Normalization considers five things together: objective difficulty, failure/entry risk, expected duration, minimum level and prerequisite depth, and permanent or physical rewards. “Under-rewarded” means the payout materially trails the nearest comparator after those burdens are considered. “Over-rewarded” requires stacked or above-band value without matching burden. Item market prices are intentionally not invented; physical samples and unlocks are reported separately.

## Categories and story function

| Category | Records | Campaign role | Runtime form |
|---|---:|---|---|

Within Operations, Acoustic Discipline/Contact form the reconnaissance-and-contact thread; Forward Reserve feeds logistics into Route Security; Low Profile/Mobility Doctrine/Heavy Assault form the equipment-and-mobility thread; Borrowed Access connects retained keys to a physical room; Contractor Intercept and Observation Window converge into Break the Perimeter; Internal Security closes the current wave using the Clearance gate.

## Campaign graph

- **Access:** Fundamentals (level 1) is the hub. Factory, Customs, Woods, Interchange, Shoreline, Reserve, and Lighthouse branch directly from it. Laboratory joins Reserve + Lighthouse. Clearance follows Laboratory. This is broad map access progression rather than a linear ten-step key ladder.
- **Arsenal:** seven independent chains—Sidearms, SMG/PDW, Shotguns, Assault Rifles, Marksman/Battle Rifles, Precision Rifles, and Special Weapons. Each is Qualification → Fieldwork → Munitions; there are no cross-family edges.
- **Operations roots:** Acoustic Discipline, Forward Reserve, and Low Profile. Mobility Doctrine follows Low Profile. Borrowed Access follows Access: Customs. Acoustic Contact follows Acoustic Discipline. Route Security joins Forward Reserve + Borrowed Access. Contractor Intercept joins Route Security + Access: Reserve. Observation Window joins Acoustic Contact + Precision Fieldwork. Heavy Assault follows Mobility Doctrine. Break the Perimeter joins Contractor Intercept + Observation Window. Internal Security joins Heavy Assault + Access: Clearance.

## Missing campaign categories and bounded additions

The current campaign has access readiness, broad weapon kills, equipment-constrained survival/combat, a small common-item handover, room visitation, distance shooting, and Scav/PMC/Rogue/Raider targets. It lacks medical/logistics recovery, reconnaissance without kills, placement/marking, weapon assembly/maintenance, boss/follower/cultist encounters, injury/survival recovery, multi-map route work, and meaningful faction-specific choice.

Recommended future additions, after this audit is accepted and without treating them as automatic graph changes:

1. **Reconnaissance and placement:** visit/mark/observe objectives with extraction, reducing reliance on kill counters.
2. **Medical readiness:** small, explicit, economically audited consumable pools; avoid rare FIR shopping lists.
3. **Weapon maintenance and assembly:** capability checks that use durability, configuration, or handover semantics instead of another elimination ladder.
4. **Selective hostile-specialist operations:** one bounded boss/follower/cultist milestone per narrative need, avoiding per-map escalation ladders.
5. **Route and recovery work:** multi-map exploration or retrieval with truthful same-raid/separate-raid wording.
6. **Faction-aware contracts:** USEC/BEAR or target-specific branches only where both sides retain a complete progression path.

Stash expansion and third-party weapon mods remain a separate research workstream. They are not inputs to the 43-record assessment or to the recommended native weapon rotation.
